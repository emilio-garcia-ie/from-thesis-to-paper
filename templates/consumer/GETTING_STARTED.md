# Start your FTTP workspace

This workspace is the writable home for one paper. Your thesis and historical
run artifacts remain outside it in the read-only paths you approve during
intake.

1. Open this folder in your selected agent.
2. Read `.fttp/guides/ONBOARDING_RATIONALE.md` and
   `.fttp/guides/USER_APPROVAL_GATES.md`.
3. Ask the agent: `Start FTTP intake for this workspace. Explain why each
   question matters before asking it. Do not access external sources, upload
   files, or approve an artifact without my explicit confirmation.`
4. Run `fttp status` to see setup work and `fttp doctor` after you add
   read-only sources or venue details.

The generated hooks intentionally fail until you replace them with your real
research steps. A successful pipeline requires a non-empty PDF; it does not
certify scientific correctness.
